## Settled (22 Oct 2026)

The user ruled that the spec wins each conflict. Each is listed with the rule it sets aside and how it is drawn.

1. **Skeleton card**, "no shimmer, no sweep, no fade: the only loop is the status-dot pulse", against "shimmer" for the placeholder fill. Drawn static, as before: #1398 asks for a placeholder fill (CL4) and names no motion. The footer line's dot is the only pulse. A shimmer, if wanted, is one change to the Skeleton part.
2. **`e3-ui-components/CLAUDE.md`**, "Body placeholders sit in fixed 40px rows". Settled to the component's own pitch: Sheet 36, Plan 42, People 52, Calendar Day 48 an hour, Allocation Timeline 36.
3. **`e3-ui-components/CLAUDE.md:591-596`**, "Keep the footer to the legend". Settled to the user's ruling: one compact line in the footer, in its own trailing slot; the legend stays whole beside it.
4. **Skeleton card**, "shown after --dur-base so quick loads never flash" (200 ms). Settled to CL4: shown after 150 ms, held at least 300 ms; cold load keeps its skeleton with no timeout.
5. **Skeleton card**, **Row-level states card**, "a failed load becomes an error Banner". Settled to CL11, "one line at the top of the failed run or region", said once: in a grid with rows, one line in the failed region's first row in view, spanning that row's cells (the Sheet's and Plan's runs, the Roster's week, the Allocation's day), never a box across rows; where no row can hold a line, the Schematic's message box at the region's top-left (the Schematic's canvas, the Calendar's month and time grids). The footer's loading line says the same, with Retry, for every failure, runs included.
6. **Skeleton card**, "static --paper-3 shapes" everywhere. Settled to the region wash, `--ink` at 6%, where items' positions aren't known yet; `--paper-3` shapes where the slot is known.
7. **Toolbar card** (proposed), "the Loading Status takes the slot of the count it will change", and **Slice summary & legend** (proposed), "the Status holds N of M's slot". Settled to CL3: one loading line in the footer; the toolbar's count shows "…" until it lands.
8. **Slice summary & legend card**, the Plan's "4,000 loaded of 18,240" and "elements 1–4,000 of 18,240", the paged Table's "4,000 of 18,240 loaded". Settled to CL3: removed.
9. **History bar card** (proposed), "0 issues: no button and no reserved slot". Settled to CL1: the slot is held from cold load, empty at 0.
10. **base-components › Collection density**, "Sheet: one size, 32px cells". Settled to the spec's pitch: 36 at md, 27 sm, 42 lg, 45 on a coarse pointer. Now drawn at 36.
11. **base-components › Grids, boards & time**, the Run bar's and Time axis's 36 lane. Settled to the spec's Plan rows: 42 two-line, 32 by kind, 88 for an open chart.
12. **Radii**, "Nothing is pill-shaped", against the spec's "a pill at the chip's height". Drawn as before: the Chip's own rect at the chip's height, `--r-sm`. If "pill" means fully rounded, every chip placeholder changes.
13. **Sheet cell card**, the enum as text and `▾` with no dot. Settled to the spec: a dot and a short bar.
14. **base-components › Status**, the Loading Status as a dot and an uppercase word. Settled to the spec's line copy: the dot and a sentence in the footer's voice.
15. **base-components › Buttons**, "Link/ghost — navigation only". Settled to the spec's Retry in the footer's loading line, drawn as the footer band's link, like its "clear all".
16. **Row-level states card**, "rows to fill the page, at most 10". Settled to CL2: the run is drawn wherever it meets the viewport, at the real count.

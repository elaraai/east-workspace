Collections.Matrix

Generic row × column grid primitive. Cells are rendered via a free slot — anything from a single weight bar to multi-segment splits, vertical capacity bars, badges, or icons. Row headers can be rich (avatar + name + sub). Configurable: orientation, brush selection, emphasis ring, corner overlays. ValueMatrixEditor, capacity heatmaps, allocation grids, and eligibility tables are all configurations of this. Matrix opts out of the Planner DnD grammar — it doesn't author add / move / remove / resize events. The cell's slot may render internal interactions (drag-to-resize a segment within a cell, brush-select across cells), but those are slot-owned and never cross cell boundaries.

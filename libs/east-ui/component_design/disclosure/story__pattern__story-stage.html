Story.Stage Anchor

The sticky slot that holds one keyframe per step. A keyframe is an ordinary east-ui subtree; the renderer owns the motion between adjacent keyframes. Authors write four plain charts — the renderer makes them one evolving chart.

Tween contract (renderer-owned): Same mark family, same x-type → continuous tween: scales/domains animate 360ms ease-out; series are matched by name — matched series tween geometry, entering series fade+draw in, exiting series fade out first. Annotations (refLine / refBand / refDot) enter by sliding from their axis, 240ms, after series settle. Different component kinds (Line → Bar, Chart → Table) → crossfade 200ms, old out then new in, frame size constant. Scrubbed properties — a keyframe may read the progress binding (Float 0–1 within the step) for scroll-locked effects: progressive data reveal (Chart.window(p)), a sweeping refLine. Scrub maps 1:1 to scroll — no easing, no duration; easing a scrub makes the chart lag the reader's hand. Reduced motion — prefers-reduced-motion ⇒ every transition becomes a 120ms crossfade and scrubbed reveals render complete.
Discipline: Keep one visual anchor across keyframes. Adjacent keyframes should share an axis, a series, or a frame region — a story where every beat swaps the whole visual is a Carousel wearing a trenchcoat. Framing belongs to keyframes, not the stage. Keyframes own their full rectangle; one that wants a border composes <Card>. Keep adjacent keyframes' framing consistent — all bare or all carded — so the stage reads as one object while it tweens, and give bare stages ≥ 32px clearance from surrounding page chrome. The stage never carries chrome. No Slice affordances, no toolbars inside the stage — the narrative is the filter. If the reader needs to explore, the story's last step links out to the Observe surface with the slice pre-bound.

Collections.Workbench

Assembly surface for product-blending and batching decisions — pulling proportions from many sources into one or more targets. Sources can be raw lots, intermediates, recipe components, fuel cuts, feed streams, ingredient SKUs — anything where the arrangement is what proportion of what. Pairs with Collections.Library on the left and a live composition for each Target on the right; a compact flow strip between them shows what's been drawn from where. One pattern, three render modes: single (N=1, focus), compare (N=2, side-by-side with auto diff), portfolio (N≥3, horizontal scroll — day-plan / shift-plan).

§2.2 Explain

2.2.7ActualVsPredictedChart

Scatter or time-series overlay of predicted vs observed for past decisions, with residual band and optional bias-line fit. Click-through to the originating decision in AuditTrail. Colour rules: identity (y=x) is dashed-grey, residual band tints brand, bias line is orange when nonzero.

ActualVsPredictedChart.Root({ points, view?: "scatter" | "timeseries", residualBand?, onPointSelect? })
scatter Predicted (x) vs actual (y) · y=x identity line
Predicted vs actual demand · last 90 days
241 decisions · MAE 4.1u · bias +1.8%
decision outlier y = x bias
timeseries Predicted (line) overlaid with actual (dots) over time
Demand forecast · 30-day rolling
predicted (line) · actual (dots) · residual band ± MAE
predicted actual residual band
click-through onPointSelect routes to AuditTrail entry for that decision
★ Behaviour. Each point may carry a decisionId; clicking opens the corresponding AuditTrail entry in a side panel. Hover surfaces predicted/actual/residual/at without committing to navigation. Outliers are tinted orange when |residual| > 2σ.

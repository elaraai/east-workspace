§2.5 Configure — part A · form-level

Configure-mode patterns for editing the inputs that drive a view: assumptions, parameter forms, guardrails, sum checks, calendar cells, presets, value grids, and sensitivity workbenches. Edits emit patches; the parent composes a draft until commit.

AssumptionsBar ParameterFormSection GuardrailNotice SumCheckBadge CalendarHeatmap PresetPicker ValueMatrixEditor SensitivityView

Continued in part B · allocation & flow — AssignmentBoard, UnassignedTray, SourceLibrary, ConflictAnnotator, SwapRequest, SupplyDemandView, DraftPublishBar.

Decision.Handle Architecture

Builds as Decision.bind in e3-ui, composing Data.bind views (decision sources ⊕ patches, staged judgements). Generic over the solution’s constraint contract — a by-name VariantType declared once in the solution package, imported by both the reasoning tasks (which $.matchTag injected constraints, fully typed) and the surface: Decision.bind([RosterConstraint], …). The public Decide API is the handle plus two surfaces: DecisionQueue (the anchor) and DecisionJournal (the read-back); everything else on this page is an internal section of the queue’s expanded row.

One binding per surface, bound once at its boundary. Decisions arrive from multiple reasoning tasks — one task optimises the roster, another the orders — each emitting its own read-only ArrayType(DecisionType) output, each bound with its own patch overlay (the standard diff binding: the patch input durably records the operator's changes against the recomputed source, with staleness surfaced by the binding's status and reviewed via Diff). The handle unions the per-source views (source ⊕ patch) into one queue and routes writes to the owning source by case id. Alongside the sources it carries the case selection and the staged judgement per case (prompt answers · knowledge · injected constraints · verdict). Resolving with Apply / Reject writes the verdict to the judgement and the case's removal through the owning patch — the row leaves because the bound view no longer contains it; on the next run the tasks read the judgements (fold injected constraints in, retire resolved cases) and the overlay reconciles. Every Decide pattern below is a pure projection of the handle; every interaction is a write through it; consequences are observed, never short-circuited. After resolve, the selection clears and the loop closes back to Triage. The seven-station loop and its back-edges are specified in §3 Use cases.

Decision.Queue Anchor

Built: DecisionQueue in e3-ui — the Decide surface. One queue, hundreds of cases; the case view is the row’s expanded state, not a sibling component.

The queue of decisions waiting on the user — the primary Decide interface. Sorted by urgency; routine items collapse with a single bulk-accept affordance. The row stays one line (urgency · kind · title · value · actions); selecting it opens one compact facet at a time below it — the row’s action buttons are the tabs, the open one pressed: Evidence (the model’s argument — evidence chips + the per-decision detail canvas slot), Options (the ranked alternatives), Judgement (prompts · knowledge · lever builder — pulses while the gate is closed), Modify (the per-kind probe editor). Opening a facet closes the previous one; opening another row closes this one (selection is the expanded row). Apply / Reject stay on the row, Apply gated by the judgement: resolving writes the verdict to the staged judgement and the removal to the owning patch overlay, and the row animates out (success wash sweeping right on apply, danger wash sweeping left on reject; a quiet fade for removals arriving from elsewhere). At scale the rows virtualize — only the open facet renders heavy content — and the header Filter is Slice composition narrowing the bound cases.

Decision.Journal Anchor

Append-only log of every consequential change. Each entry is timestamped, attributed, and linked to the run, diff, and rationale. The system's memory of what happened, who decided it, and why. Owned by the Decide family — builds as DecisionJournal in e3-ui over the surface's judgements binding: the resolved cases (verdict, rationale, injected constraints, resolution time) are the journal's decision entries; the full multi-actor feed (model issues, system detections) layers on later.

Stakes.Scope

The blast radius of the decision — who and what is affected if it goes wrong. Pairs with Stakes.Tag: tag answers how severe, scope answers how far. Renders as a compact icon-keyed strip; each axis is independently clickable to drill into the affected set.

Scope5 axes · click to drill
Order is fixed: people → teams → window → $ → downstream. Empty axes render dimmed, not omitted.
